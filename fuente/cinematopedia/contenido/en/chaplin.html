A bowler hat, a bamboo cane, baggy trousers, a tight jacket and huge shoes. With that improvised costume {n:charles-chaplin|Charles Chaplin} created the Tramp in 1914 and became the most famous person on the planet.

{fig:img/r/Q882.webp|n/charles-chaplin.html|Charles Chaplin.}

A childhood of poverty

Chaplin was born in London in 1889, into a family of music hall performers. His father, an alcoholic, died young, and his mother suffered mental illness that led to her being committed. Charles and his brother Sydney spent periods in workhouses. He performed on stage from childhood, and with a comedy company, Fred Karno's, he travelled to the United States.

The birth of the Tramp

In 1913 he was hired by the Keystone studio, and the following year the Tramp appeared for the first time. The character was such a success that within a few years Chaplin was earning a fortune and controlling his own films. In 1919 he founded United Artists with Mary Pickford, Douglas Fairbanks and D. W. Griffith.

Laughter and tears

What set Chaplin apart was his ability to mix comedy and emotion. {p:el-chico-1921|The Kid} (1921) told the story of the Tramp and an abandoned child. In {p:la-quimera-del-oro-1925|The Gold Rush} (1925) he ate a boiled boot as if it were a delicacy. When sound arrived, Chaplin resisted: {p:luces-de-la-ciudad-1931|City Lights} (1931) and {p:tiempos-modernos-1936|Modern Times} (1936) remained almost silent.

The Great Dictator

In 1940 he released {p:el-gran-dictador-1940|The Great Dictator}, his first talking picture, in which he mocked Hitler while the United States was not yet at war. It ended with a long speech in favour of humanity and democracy.

His political views caused him trouble during the witch-hunt years, and in 1952, while he was travelling to Europe to present {p:candilejas-1952|Limelight}, his permit to return to the United States was revoked. He settled in Switzerland. In 1972 he went back to Hollywood to receive an honorary Oscar and one of the longest standing ovations in the history of the ceremony. He died on Christmas Day 1977.
