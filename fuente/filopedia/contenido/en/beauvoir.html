«One is not born, but rather becomes, a woman.» Few philosophical sentences have been quoted so often. {a:simone-de-beauvoir|Simone de Beauvoir} wrote it in 1949, in a book that changed the way people think about women.

{fig:img/r/Q7197.jpg|a/simone-de-beauvoir.html|Simone de Beauvoir. Image: Wikimedia Commons.}

A brilliant student

Beauvoir was born in Paris on 9 January 1908, into a bourgeois Catholic family that was gradually losing its money. From an early age she decided she wanted to earn her own living and think for herself. She studied philosophy at the Sorbonne, and in 1929 she passed the agrégation, a very demanding competitive exam, with the second-highest mark. First place went to {a:jean-paul-sartre|Jean-Paul Sartre}, with whom she had a lifelong relationship, without ever marrying or living together.

Novelist and philosopher

Her first novel, {o:la-invitada|She Came to Stay}, was published in 1943 and portrays a love triangle. Years later she won the Prix Goncourt for {o:los-mandarines|The Mandarins} (1954), about French intellectuals after the Second World War. Alongside Sartre she was one of the great voices of {c:existencialismo|existentialism}, the movement which holds that we have no fixed essence and that we make ourselves through our choices.

«The Second Sex»

In {o:el-segundo-sexo|The Second Sex} she applied that idea to women. Her argument is that society has always defined woman in relation to man, as «the Other», and that many things considered natural in women were in fact learned. To write it she drew on biology, history, psychoanalysis, myth and literature.

The book caused a scandal. Thousands of copies sold in the first week, and it was harshly attacked. In time it became a key work of {c:feminismo|feminism} in the second half of the twentieth century.

Her legacy

Beauvoir also wrote memoirs, essays on old age and travel writing. She died in Paris on 14 April 1986 and is buried beside Sartre in Montparnasse cemetery. Her underlying question is still open: how much of what we are is given, and how much do we build?
