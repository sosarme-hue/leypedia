Planning a wedding can feel overwhelming at first: dozens of decisions, suppliers, deadlines and a budget to balance. The key is to spread the work over time. With about twelve months ahead of you, everything can be done calmly. Here is a month-by-month guide.

12 to 10 months before

  • Set a total budget and agree on who contributes what. We explain how in {art:presupuesto|how to plan your wedding budget}.
  • Draw up a first guest list. The number of guests shapes almost everything else.
  • Choose the date and the {u:venues/|venue}. The most popular places, especially on Saturdays from May to October, are booked a year or more ahead.
  • If you want professional help, now is the time to hire a {u:wedding-and-event-planners/|wedding planner}.

10 to 8 months before

  • Book {u:photography-and-video/|photography and video}, {u:catering/|catering} (if the venue does not include it) and {u:music-and-entertainment/|music}. Good professionals have full diaries too.
  • Start looking for the dress or suit in {u:bridal-wear/|bridal wear} shops: a made-to-measure dress can take six months or more.
  • Send a save-the-date if guests are coming from far away.

7 to 5 months before

  • Book {u:flowers-and-decor/|flowers and decor}, the {u:cakes-and-pastry/|cake} and, if needed, a {u:tents-and-furniture-hire/|marquee and furniture}.
  • For a civil wedding in Spain, ask at the civil registry or town hall early: the paperwork can take weeks or months.
  • Reserve accommodation for out-of-town guests and think about transport.

4 to 2 months before

  • Send the invitations, with a deadline to reply.
  • Do the menu tasting with the caterer.
  • Choose the rings and prepare the ceremony: readings, music, who will speak.

The final month

  • Confirm the final number of guests and the seating plan.
  • Confirm timings with every supplier and write a timeline for the day.
  • Final dress fitting and hair trial.
  • Delegate: someone you trust should be the suppliers' contact on the day.

What if we have less time?

A wedding can be organised in six months, or even less, if you are flexible with the date: a Friday, a Sunday or an off-season wedding opens many doors. In that case, {u:get-quotes/|ask several venues for quotes} at once to save time.
