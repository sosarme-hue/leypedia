Farmhouses and country estates, known in Catalonia as masías, are among the favourite places for a wedding in Spain: outdoor space, stone walls, gardens and the chance to hold everything in one place, from the ceremony to the dancing. But they are not all the same. These are the questions to ask before you book.

{fig:estatico/fotos/masia.jpg|farmhouses-and-estates/|A farmhouse set up for a wedding banquet. Photo: celebraciones.org}

Capacity and spaces

Ask for the minimum and maximum number of guests, and whether there are separate areas for the ceremony, drinks, dinner and party. A place that is too big for few guests can feel empty; one that is too small, uncomfortable.

Plan B if it rains

This is the most important question for an outdoor wedding. Is there an indoor hall for everyone? Can a marquee be put up? Who decides to switch, and how far in advance?

In-house or outside catering

Some estates have their own exclusive caterer; others let you choose. In-house catering is simpler but less flexible. If you can choose, there may be a kitchen fee. We explain more in {art:catering|how to choose the wedding menu and caterer}.

Timings and noise

Many venues have a finishing time set by local rules or neighbours, and volume limits for music. Ask how late you can dance and whether the music can continue indoors.

Exclusivity

Will there be another wedding on the same day? Some large estates hold two events at once in separate areas. If you want the place to yourselves, ask whether exclusive use is included.

What the price includes

  • Furniture, linen, tableware and set-up.
  • Lighting, a {u:sound-and-lighting/|sound system} and electricity.
  • Parking, accessibility and enough toilets.
  • Rooms for the couple or the guests, if any.
  • Security, cleaning and overtime.

Getting there

Many farmhouses are in the countryside, far from public transport. Think about how guests will arrive and leave: do you need a coach? Are taxis available in the early hours?

Visit in person

Photos can be misleading. Visit the estate, if possible at the time of day of the wedding, to see the light and the atmosphere. In our {u:farmhouses-and-estates/|farmhouses and estates} section you can compare them by area and {u:get-quotes/|ask for quotes} with no obligation.
