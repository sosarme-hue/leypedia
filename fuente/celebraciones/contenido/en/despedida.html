A hen or stag party no longer has to be a night of fancy dress and bar crawls. More and more groups want something different: an experience everyone enjoys and remembers fondly. Here are ideas for every taste and budget.

Think about the person getting married

The golden rule: the party is for the person getting married, not for the group. Do they love adventure or calm? Surprises or being in control? What would they hate? From there, everything is easier.

For adventurers

Canyoning, rafting, kayaking, paintball, quad bikes or climbing. {u:hen-stag-activities/|Adventure activities} bring the group together and are remembered for years. They combine well with a lunch or dinner.

At sea

A {u:hen-stag-boat-trips/|boat or catamaran trip}, with swimming, music and food on board, is one of the most popular options on the Spanish coast from April to October.

To unwind

A {u:hen-stag-spa/|spa day}, with a thermal circuit, massages and healthy food, is perfect for groups who prefer something calm, or as a recovery morning after the party.

Dinner and a show

A {u:hen-stag-dinner-shows/|dinner with a show}, stand-up comedy, cabaret or karaoke, followed by dancing. The classic, done well.

More ideas

  • A workshop together: cooking, wine tasting, pottery or cocktails.
  • An escape room or a treasure hunt around the city with challenges about the guest of honour.
  • A weekend in a country house with a barbecue and games.
  • A wine route or a winery visit.

Practical tips

  • Hold it at least two or three weeks before the wedding, not the night before.
  • Agree a budget per person from the start and stick to it.
  • No pranks that could humiliate anyone or put them at risk.
  • If you don't want to organise it all, {u:hen-stag-organisers/|party organisers} can arrange everything for a fixed price.
