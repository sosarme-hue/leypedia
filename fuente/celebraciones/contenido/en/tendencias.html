Weddings change a little every year: new colours, new customs, new ideas. What will we see in 2027? Here is our pick of the trends already reaching many weddings.

{fig:estatico/fotos/bodas.jpg|suppliers/|An outdoor wedding. Photo: celebraciones.org}

Smaller, more personal weddings

More couples prefer fewer guests and more attention to detail. Micro-weddings, with under fifty people, let you spend more per guest and enjoy each one.

Multi-day celebrations

A wedding is no longer just one day: a welcome dinner the night before, the wedding and a brunch the next morning. It works very well at {u:farmhouses-and-estates/|farmhouses and estates} with accommodation.

Sustainability

  • Seasonal, locally grown flowers, or plants that can be replanted afterwards.
  • Menus with local produce.
  • Digital invitations or recycled paper.
  • Second-hand or rented dresses.

Food as an experience

Live cooking stations, wine pairings and chef's menus. {u:catering/|Catering} takes centre stage, as we explain in {art:catering|how to choose the wedding menu and caterer}.

Colours

Earthy tones, terracotta and sage green remain popular, and bold colours are growing: burgundy, deep blue and butter yellow.

More video, fewer phones

"Unplugged" ceremonies, where guests are asked to put their phones away, are increasingly common. In return, more couples hire professional {u:photography-and-video/|video}, sometimes with short clips to share on the day.

Party till the end

Live bands, DJs and entertainment. The party keeps getting longer, with a late-night snack. Find options in {u:music-and-entertainment/|music and entertainment}.

And you?

Trends are ideas, not rules. The best wedding is the one that feels like you.
