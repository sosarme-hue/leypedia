The company Christmas dinner is a tradition many look forward to and some dread. Well organised, it brings the team together and ends the year on a high. If it's your turn to plan it this year, here are ideas and a checklist. And hurry: the best December dates go fast.

First, the basic questions

  • How many people? Send a quick poll to find out who is coming.
  • What budget? Ask how much the company pays per person and whether drinks are included.
  • Lunch or dinner? A lunch is easier for people with families and usually cheaper. A dinner has more atmosphere.
  • Which day? Thursdays and Fridays in mid-December are the most in demand. A Monday or Tuesday gives more choice and better prices.

Where to hold it

For small groups, a {u:restaurants/|restaurant} with a private room is simplest. For large groups, {u:banquet-and-party-halls/|banquet and party halls} and {u:hotels/|hotels} offer set menus, a dance floor and sometimes an open bar. A {u:farmhouses-and-estates/|farmhouse or estate} is a different option for a big group.

Ideas so it's not the usual dinner

  • A workshop before dinner: cooking, wine tasting or cocktails.
  • A team activity, such as an escape room or a treasure hunt.
  • A short magic or comedy show.
  • A Secret Santa with a price limit and a sense of humour.
  • A photo booth with Christmas props.
  • A charity raffle or a food collection at the dinner itself.

Make everyone feel included

Ask about allergies, intolerances and vegetarian or vegan diets in good time. Think about people who don't drink alcohol and offer good alternatives. And choose a place with good transport links or arrange transport home: nobody should drive after the party.

Timeline

  • Now: choose the date and book the venue.
  • Two weeks before: confirm numbers and the menu.
  • One week before: confirm timings, transport and activities.
