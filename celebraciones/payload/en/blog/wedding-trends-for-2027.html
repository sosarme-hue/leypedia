Wedding trends for 2027

12 January 2027 · 4 min read · By Alejandro Rosàs

Smaller weddings, multi-day celebrations, sustainability and more: what is coming.

Weddings change a little every year: new colours, new customs, new ideas. What will we see in 2027? Here is our pick of the trends already reaching many weddings.

Smaller, more personal weddings

More couples prefer fewer guests and more attention to detail. Micro-weddings, with under fifty people, let you spend more per guest and enjoy each one.

Multi-day celebrations

A wedding is no longer just one day: a welcome dinner the night before, the wedding and a brunch the next morning. It works very well at farmhouses and estates with accommodation.

Sustainability

  • Seasonal, locally grown flowers, or plants that can be replanted afterwards.
  • Menus with local produce.
  • Digital invitations or recycled paper.
  • Second-hand or rented dresses.

Food as an experience

Live cooking stations, wine pairings and chef's menus. Catering takes centre stage, as we explain in how to choose the wedding menu and caterer.

Colours

Earthy tones, terracotta and sage green remain popular, and bold colours are growing: burgundy, deep blue and butter yellow.

More video, fewer phones

"Unplugged" ceremonies, where guests are asked to put their phones away, are increasingly common. In return, more couples hire professional video, sometimes with short clips to share on the day.

Party till the end

Live bands, DJs and entertainment. The party keeps getting longer, with a late-night snack. Find options in music and entertainment.

And you?

Trends are ideas, not rules. The best wedding is the one that feels like you.
