Proposing at Christmas: ideas

29 December 2026 · 4 min read · By Alejandro Rosàs

Ideas and tips for an unforgettable proposal this holiday season.

Christmas is one of the favourite times of year to propose: lights, family gatherings and a special atmosphere. But it's also easy to fall into clichés. If you're thinking of popping the question this holiday season, here are ideas and some tips to make it go well.

First: know your partner

Would they like an intimate proposal, just the two of you, or surrounded by family and friends? Some people love a public surprise and others find it very uncomfortable. Choose with them in mind, not social media.

Ideas for a Christmas proposal

  • A walk through your city's Christmas lights, and the question in the prettiest spot.
  • Dinner at a special restaurant, with the ring at dessert (or better, handed over: some rings have been swallowed).
  • A weekend away at a country house with a fireplace, or in the snow.
  • On the Christmas tree, with the ring hung like a decoration or in a box among the presents.
  • A personalised Advent calendar with a shared memory each day, and the question on the last.
  • With the family, at Christmas Eve dinner or Christmas lunch, if your partner would like that.
  • At midnight on New Year's Eve, right after the chimes.

Practical tips

  • If you don't know the ring size, ask someone you trust or choose an adjustable design.
  • If you want photos, a photographer can hide nearby and capture the moment.
  • Have a plan B in case of rain or crowds.
  • Afterwards, celebrate: book a table or plan a small party with your closest people.
