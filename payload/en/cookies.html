Cookies

Which cookies this site uses

Classicapedia uses two kinds of Google cookies, and only if you accept them in Google's consent notice shown when you arrive:

  • Analytics (Google Analytics): to see, in aggregate, how many people visit the site and which pages they read.
  • Advertising (Google AdSense): the site shows ads to cover its costs. Google and its partners use cookies to show ads, limit repetition, measure results and, if you agree, personalise them.

If you do not give consent, Google Analytics is not loaded and Google only shows non-personalised or limited ads.

Changing or withdrawing your consent

You can change your mind at any time with the Cookie settings link, which is also at the foot of every page. You can also delete or block cookies in your browser settings.

Third-party content

Portraits loaded from Wikimedia Commons make your browser connect to the Wikimedia Foundation's servers, which apply their own privacy policy. YouTube videos are not loaded until you press play; from then on YouTube's terms apply (privacy-enhanced mode, youtube-nocookie.com).
