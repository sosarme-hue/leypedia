About Classicapedia

Who is behind Classicapedia

Classicapedia is an independent personal project created and maintained by Alejandro Rosàs Navarro. He also founded and runs Classical Archive (classicalarchive.org) and its network of Facebook communities devoted to classical music.

My passion for classical music began at the age of twelve and has never left me. I am not a professional musician: I am a family physician. I learned as a self-taught listener, through more than fifty years of listening, reading, collecting recordings and discovering the lives of composers and performers.

Why Classicapedia

Classical Archive was created to preserve and connect the history of classical music: its recordings, documents, photographs and books. Classicapedia is its companion. It is a free, bilingual biographical encyclopedia where anyone can find out in seconds who a composer, conductor, singer, instrumentalist or instrument maker was. It also explains why that person matters.

Its more than 22,000 biographies are written specifically for this site from standard musicological reference sources. They run from the Middle Ages to the present day and include both the great names and many forgotten figures who deserve to be remembered.

A community

Classicapedia is part of an international community of music lovers that meets every day on Facebook to share recordings, documents and discoveries. Taking part is open and free.

Independent and free

The site is free and has no commercial agenda. To cover its costs it shows some Google ads, which have no influence on the content. If you would like to help keep it running and growing, you can do so through Patreon. Your support covers hosting, technical development and new content.

If you spot a mistake or would like to suggest a biography, write to info@classicapedia.com.

The family of sites

Classicapedia belongs to a family of free encyclopedias by the same author:

  • classicapedia.com, the encyclopedia of classical music (this site).
  • pinacopedia.com, the history of painting.
  • filopedia.com, thought and letters.
  • cinematopedia.com, film history.
  • medicapedia.org, the history of medicine.
  • tenispedia.com, tennis history.
  • newtonpedia.com, the history of science and technology.
