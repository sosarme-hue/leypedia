Privacy

Data controller

The data controller is Alejandro Rosàs Navarro, who can be reached at info@classicapedia.com.

What data is processed

This site has no forms or user accounts. The following data is processed:

  • Server logs. Hostinger, the hosting provider, keeps the IP address, date and requested page for a limited time. It does so for security and for the technical running of the site, on the basis of legitimate interest.
  • Emails you send me. I use them only to reply and never pass them on. They are kept only as long as needed to deal with your message.
  • Visit statistics. Only if you agree in the cookie notice, Google Analytics (Google Ireland Ltd.) collects aggregated browsing data: pages viewed, device type, approximate country. The legal basis is your consent, which you can withdraw at any time under Cookies. Google may process this data outside the EU under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. More in the cookie policy.
  • Advertising. To cover its costs, the site shows Google AdSense ads (Google Ireland Ltd.). Google and its advertising partners may use cookies and identifiers to show ads, limit how often you see the same one, measure results and, only if you agree, personalise them to your interests. We ask you through Google's consent notice when you arrive, and you can change your choice at any time with the Cookie settings link. The legal basis is your consent; if you do not give it, Google only shows non-personalised or limited ads. More on how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Third-party content

Many portraits are loaded directly from Wikimedia Commons (upload.wikimedia.org). When they are shown, your browser connects to the Wikimedia Foundation's servers, which receive your IP address and apply their own privacy policy. Ads are served by Google from its own servers. The Facebook and Patreon links only connect you to those services if you click them.

Your rights

You can request access to, rectification or erasure of your data, or restrict or object to its processing, by writing to info@classicapedia.com. If you believe your rights have not been respected, you can complain to the Spanish Data Protection Agency (aepd.es).
