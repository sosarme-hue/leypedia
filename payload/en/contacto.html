Contact

For any question, correction or suggestion for a biography, write to info@classicapedia.com. I read and answer every email personally, usually within a few days.

Before you write

  • Mistakes in a biography: give the person's name or the page address, and what needs correcting. A source helps.
  • Suggesting a new biography: tell me the name and why you think it belongs here.
  • Images and rights: if you hold the rights to an image and want it removed or credited differently, I will do it as soon as you write.
  • Privacy: to exercise your data rights, write to the same address (more under Privacy).
