Is my pet sick? Signs you should not ignore

Emergency now

Struggling to breathe, pale gums, swollen hard belly, seizures, ate something toxic.

Book a vet today

Vomiting or diarrhoea for over a day, not eating for 24 h (cats) or 48 h (dogs), limping, drinking much more than usual.

Watch at home

A single vomit, sneezing without fever, a little less appetite but in good spirits.

General guidance only. If in doubt, call your vet. 🚑 Emergency vets in Spain →
