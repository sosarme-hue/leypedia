Odin, the god who gave an eye for wisdom

19 November 2026 · 5 min read · By Alejandro Rosàs

One-eyed, a wanderer and lord of Valhalla: the father of the Norse gods gave everything to know more.

He is the father of the Norse gods, the lord of Asgard and the wisest of them all. But he is also an unsettling god: one-eyed, a wanderer, a friend of the dead and willing to trick anyone to get what he wants. Odin is one of the most fascinating figures in the mythology of northern Europe.

An eye for wisdom

According to the Prose Edda, beneath one of the roots of the great world ash, Yggdrasil, there was a well whose waters gave wisdom. It was guarded by the wise Mímir. Odin wanted to drink from it, and the price was one of his eyes. He gave it without hesitation. That is why he is almost always shown with one eye, a broad-brimmed hat and a grey cloak.

Nine nights on the tree

In the poem «Hávamál», preserved in the Poetic Edda, Odin tells how he hung for nine nights on a wind-swept tree, wounded by his own spear, without food or drink, sacrificed «himself to himself». At the end of the ordeal he saw the runes and took them up. That is how he won the secret of writing and magic.

His companions

Two ravens, Huginn («thought») and Muninn («memory»), fly over the world every day and whisper in his ear what they have seen. He rides Sleipnir, an eight-legged horse, and wields Gungnir, a spear that never misses. Two wolves eat from his table, because he lives on wine alone.

Lord of Valhalla

The valkyries ride over battlefields and choose half of the fallen warriors to take them to Valhalla, Odin's great hall. There they train and feast while they wait for Ragnarök, the end of the world. Odin knows that in that final battle he will be swallowed by the wolf Fenrir, and still he prepares for it.

His mark today

In English, Wednesday is Woden's day, Woden being Odin's name in Old English. His figure as an old wise man with a hat and a beard inspired, by Tolkien's own account, the wizard Gandalf, and today he appears in comics, films and video games. His best-known sons are Thor and Baldr, and his wife is Frigg.
